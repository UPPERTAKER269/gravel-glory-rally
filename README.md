# VUKA:CAR RACING

Core MVP Features

Main Menu: Play, Garage, Settings and Career Mode.

Car Selection: Choose from a small collection of rally cars.

Racing Gameplay: Real-time 3D driving with acceleration, braking, steering and drifting.

Tracks: Start with 3 different environments, each with unique roads and challenges.

AI Opponents: Race against computer-controlled drivers.

Physics: Realistic vehicle handling, suspension, drifting and collisions.

Camera Views: Third-person chase camera and cockpit camera.

Progression: Earn coins and unlock new cars and tracks.

HUD: Speedometer, timer, position indicator and minimap.

Mobile Controls: On-screen steering, brake and acceleration buttons.

MVP Goal

Deliver a playable, polished 3D rally racing experience with exciting driving physics, competitive races, and a simple progression system. The game should be optimized for Android and PC, with room for future multiplayer, more cars, larger maps and advanced customization.
